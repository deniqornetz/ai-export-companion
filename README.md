# AI Export Companion

**An AI-powered export preparation companion for Indonesian MSMEs and exporters.**

## The Problem

Indonesian MSMEs and exporters can struggle to understand destination-market requirements, identify export-readiness gaps, and determine the next practical steps toward entering international markets.

## Our Approach

AI Export Companion aims to turn export preparation into a structured, actionable workflow.

The planned experience includes:

- **Export Profile:** Capture the commodity, destination market, and basic business information.
- **Requirement Mapping:** Organize relevant export requirements and identify information that still needs verification.
- **Gap Analysis:** Highlight missing information and potential preparation gaps.
- **Action Planning:** Generate prioritized next steps tailored to the user's situation.
- **AI-Powered Guidance:** Use Meta AI models to explain complex information in accessible language while distinguishing verified facts from assumptions.

## Who It Is For

Indonesian MSMEs, first-time exporters, and experienced exporters exploring new products or destination markets.

The project is designed to support diverse commodities, including agricultural and coconut-based products.

## Responsible AI

AI-generated guidance is not a substitute for official regulations, qualified professional advice, or confirmation from importers and relevant authorities. Important requirements should be supported by reliable sources and clearly marked when they require verification.
